# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA / Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own the quality assurance strategy and ensure that deliverables meet acceptance criteria and quality standards before release. They work closely with developers and product managers to validate features and maintain product reliability.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain comprehensive test plans and test cases
- Define quality acceptance criteria with product and engineering teams
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts
- Identify, triage, and track defects throughout the development cycle
- Validate fixes and perform regression testing before release
- Advise on testability of designs and implementations
- Establish and maintain test automation frameworks

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality features with minimal production defects
- Enable fast, confident releases through comprehensive testing
- Improve test coverage and automation over time
- Reduce time spent on manual testing through strategic automation

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Collaborate on test strategies, code review for testability, and defect triage
- **Product Managers**: Define acceptance criteria and validate feature completeness
- **Project Managers**: Report on quality metrics and provide release readiness assessments
- **Technical Lead/Architect**: Consult on testing strategy for complex systems

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and kickoff meetings
- Test plan reviews with engineering and product leads
- Bug triage and defect discussions
- Release readiness reviews and quality sign-offs

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads own the overall technical strategy, system design, and engineering best practices. They mentor the development team, make key architectural decisions, and ensure the system remains scalable, maintainable, and performant.

### Responsibilities
- Design system architecture and technical approach for new features
- Guide design reviews and lead code review practices
- Mentor developers and review technical solutions for quality and best practices
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Ensure adherence to coding standards, security practices, and architectural patterns
- Collaborate with stakeholders on technical trade-offs and feasibility
- Document architectural decisions and technical standards

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable, and performant systems
- Reduce technical debt and improve code quality
- Enable rapid iteration while maintaining system stability
- Build a strong engineering culture and knowledge-sharing environment

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Provide technical guidance, mentor on best practices, and review designs
- **Product Managers**: Discuss technical feasibility and trade-offs for proposed features
- **Project Managers**: Escalate technical risks and help estimate effort for complex work
- **QA/Testing Lead**: Advise on testability and test strategy for complex systems
- **DevOps/Release Engineer**: Collaborate on scalability and deployment architecture

### Typical Communication
- Technical design discussions and architecture reviews
- Code review feedback and mentoring sessions
- Risk assessments and technical escalations
- Technical documentation and decision logs

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters and Agile Coaches facilitate team processes, remove blockers, and help teams adopt agile practices. They focus on continuous improvement and enabling the team to work efficiently while maintaining psychological safety.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint ceremonies (planning, standup, review, retrospective)
- Identify and help remove blockers and impediments
- Coach the team on agile practices and continuous improvement
- Track velocity and team capacity to support realistic planning
- Foster psychological safety and encourage feedback
- Support the team in maintaining Definition of Done
- Escalate organizational impediments to Project Manager or leadership

### Goals
- Enable consistent team velocity and delivery predictability
- Build a high-performing, self-organizing team
- Create a culture of continuous improvement and learning
- Reduce friction and maximize team productivity

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Support team collaboration and remove blockers
- **Project Managers**: Provide insights on team capacity and sprint health
- **Product Managers**: Facilitate backlog refinement and prioritization discussions
- **All Roles**: Coach on agile practices and facilitate communications

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- One-on-ones with team members
- Retrospective facilitation and action item tracking
- Escalations to Project Manager for organizational blockers

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders and Sponsors provide business context, strategic direction, and approvals for initiatives. They represent business interests, secure resources, and ensure projects align with organizational goals and priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Define business context and success criteria for initiatives
- Provide strategic direction and help prioritize among competing projects
- Approve major decisions and resource allocations
- Monitor project progress and business metrics
- Communicate project status to their organization and leadership
- Escalate business-critical issues and risks
- Support team by removing organizational blockers

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver business value and strategic alignment
- Maximize return on investment in engineering and product efforts
- Maintain executive visibility and confidence in delivery
- Support a sustainable pace and culture for the team

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Project Managers**: Primary point of contact for status, escalations, and approvals
- **Product Managers**: Align on business priorities and success metrics
- **All Roles**: Provide business context and strategic rationale for work

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates and business reviews
- Approval meetings for major initiatives and resource decisions
- Escalation pathways for critical business issues
- Strategic alignment sessions and roadmap reviews

---

## Security / Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Officers ensure that projects meet security and regulatory requirements. They establish security standards, conduct reviews, and help mitigate security and compliance risks throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Define security and compliance requirements for projects
- Conduct security design reviews and threat assessments
- Establish and maintain security standards and best practices
- Perform or coordinate security testing and vulnerability assessments
- Review code and infrastructure for security and compliance gaps
- Track and remediate security and compliance issues
- Advise on data privacy, encryption, and regulatory obligations
- Support incident response and post-incident analysis

### Goals
- Prevent security vulnerabilities and compliance violations in production
- Build security into the development process from the start
- Reduce risk to the organization and customers
- Enable fast, secure feature delivery

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Educate on security practices and review code for vulnerabilities
- **Technical Lead/Architect**: Collaborate on security architecture and design
- **QA/Testing Lead**: Define security test cases and validation strategies
- **DevOps/Release Engineer**: Ensure secure infrastructure and deployment practices
- **Project Managers**: Escalate security risks and coordinate remediation timelines

### Typical Communication
- Security design reviews and threat assessments
- Security testing and vulnerability reports
- Training and awareness sessions for the team
- Compliance audit support and security incident response

---

## DevOps / Release Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps engineers and Release Engineers manage infrastructure, deployment pipelines, and release processes. They ensure smooth, reliable deployments; maintain system observability; and enable fast, safe feature delivery to production.

### Responsibilities
- Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines and automation
- Manage infrastructure and environments (dev, staging, production)
- Plan, execute, and monitor deployments and rollouts
- Implement monitoring, logging, alerting, and observability
- Support incident response, troubleshooting, and rollback procedures
- Document deployment procedures, runbooks, and operational playbooks
- Collaborate on infrastructure scalability and capacity planning
- Ensure security and compliance in infrastructure and deployments

### Goals
- Enable fast, safe deployments with minimal manual effort and risk
- Maintain high system reliability, uptime, and performance
- Reduce deployment lead time and mean time to recovery (MTTR)
- Provide visibility and observability into system health and behavior

### Interaction with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Support deployment processes and provide observability for debugging
- **Technical Lead/Architect**: Collaborate on scalability, reliability, and architecture
- **QA/Testing Lead**: Provide test environments and support end-to-end testing
- **Project Managers**: Coordinate deployment windows and communicate deployment status
- **Security/Compliance Officer**: Ensure secure infrastructure and compliance controls

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and deployment schedules
- Infrastructure and pipeline reviews and improvements
- Incident response and post-incident reviews (blameless retrospectives)
- Monitoring, alerting, and observability configuration discussions
- Operational runbook updates and team training
